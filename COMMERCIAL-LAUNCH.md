# WYRMORA Medical — commercial launch checklist

This package is a deployable software codebase, not permission to operate a pharmacy or sell medicines. Complete each item before commercial launch.

## Required before patient-facing clinical reliance
- [ ] Appoint a Nepal-registered pharmacist/qualified clinical reviewer.
- [ ] Review every medicine record, Nepali translation, indications, contraindications, side effects and prescription status.
- [ ] Store source, edition/version, review date, reviewer identity and next review date for each record.
- [ ] Add an urgent-care notice and a way to report inaccurate/outdated medicine content.
- [ ] Ensure no personalised diagnosis, treatment or dose recommendation is generated.

## Required before showing prices as confirmed
- [ ] Confirm medicine brand, generic ingredient, strength, dosage form, pack size and manufacturer.
- [ ] Record pharmacy/supplier identity, MRP/retail basis, source and date/time of confirmation.
- [ ] Reconfirm prices regularly and expire stale prices.
- [ ] Clearly distinguish MRP from seller offer price and delivery fees.

## Required before orders or revenue
- [ ] Obtain applicable company, pharmacy, DDA and local permissions; verify current legal requirements with Nepal counsel/regulator.
- [ ] Verify all partner pharmacies and suppliers and retain licence evidence.
- [ ] Build pharmacist prescription verification and dispensing process for prescription-only medicines.
- [ ] Sign written partner contracts and define commission, refunds, delivery, recalls and complaints.
- [ ] Obtain payment gateway merchant approval and integrate signed server-side payment confirmation.
- [ ] Implement order status, delivery tracking, invoices, refunds, accounting/tax and customer support.
- [ ] Publish privacy policy, terms, consent notices, retention policy and data breach process.
- [ ] Complete security review, backups/restore test, monitoring and incident response plan.

## Revenue model to enable after approvals
- Pharmacy SaaS subscription for inventory/catalogue and order management.
- Commission on completed, legally permitted orders under pharmacy agreements.
- Paid pharmacy software features and business analytics.

Any sponsored listing must be clearly labelled, must not override clinical information, and must comply with medicine advertising restrictions.
